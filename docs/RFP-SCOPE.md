# APSS One scope — working summary
The APSS One RFP specifies 221 requirements: 138 Must, 68 Should, 15 Could.
It seeks one login, one member record and one APSS-branded member and administrator experience.

Modules to demonstrate or label *Coming Soon*:
1. Membership applications, renewals, fee/status records
2. Searchable public speaker profiles linked to eligibility
3. Community, SIGs and group discussions
4. Events, RSVP, tiered tickets, check-in
5. Annual Convention and passes
6. Professional development: 4 Core Modules, 26 electives, rubrics, CPD, mentoring
7. Email campaigns, consent and notifications
8. Payments, refunds, invoice records, Xero export
9. Public website and CMS
10. ExCo console, delegated roles, audits
11. Analytics and reports

Proposed stack: WordPress + CiviCRM + custom APSS modules. Custom modules **are not built** yet.
For formal tender compliance, respond individually to all 221 items with Y/P/O/N and notes. Do not imply the concept demo meets launch requirements.
